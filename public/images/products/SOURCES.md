# Headphone image sources

Retrieved 2026-09-04. Manufacturer product photography used to identify the products discussed in the catalogue. Copyright remains with the respective manufacturers; these are not original site photography or Creative Commons assets.

Processed locally to 640 × 640 WebP (quality 85), trimming empty borders and adding padding without changing the products. Images are served locally, with no third-party image requests. Colours are illustrative; the catalogue does not promise a specific colour variant.

All 15 files preserve transparent alpha and transparent padding. Original manufacturer transparency is used for Soundcore, AirPods Pro 2, Bowers & Wilkins and Sony WF-1000XM5. The other eleven photos were cut out locally with macOS Vision foreground masks; no generative product images were used. All cutouts were visually checked against coloured backgrounds, including the gaps inside headbands and the complete earbud cases. An alpha channel alone is insufficient: some manufacturer images include opaque white rectangles inside transparent margins.
